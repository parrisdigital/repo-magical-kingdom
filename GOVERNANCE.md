# Governance

Repo Magical Kingdom uses a maintainer-led, community-informed model. The goal
is to make decisions transparent while keeping responsibility clear during the
project's early development.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who participates constructively through issues, discussions,
documentation, code, design, testing, research, or licensed assets.

### Reviewers

Trusted contributors who regularly review a project area. Review authority may
be scoped to code, rendering, accessibility, security, documentation, or assets.

### Maintainers

People listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Maintainers merge changes,
manage releases and repository settings, enforce community policies, and are
accountable for legal and security decisions.

## Decisions

- Routine changes use pull-request review and maintainer approval.
- Substantial changes to world semantics, public contracts, privacy, licensing,
  or contributor governance begin with a public issue or design discussion.
- Accepted architectural decisions should be recorded in documentation with the
  alternatives and consequences.
- Maintainers seek rough consensus. When consensus cannot be reached, the lead
  maintainer makes and documents the final decision.

Visual metaphors carry product meaning. A change that turns an import into a
road, a test into a guardian, or a repository into a portal is an architectural
decision, not merely decoration.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer status is based on sustained, constructive contributions, sound
judgment, reliable review, and alignment with the Code of Conduct. Existing
maintainers approve additions and record them in `MAINTAINERS.md` through a
pull request.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes require a public pull request, an explanation of the need,
and approval from the lead maintainer. Security-sensitive details may be
handled privately before a public summary is published.
